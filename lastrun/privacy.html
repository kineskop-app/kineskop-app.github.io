Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Last Run (“Останній рейс”) is a game by Kineskop for iPhone and Android. We don't collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress in all save slots, the choices you made, the endings you've unlocked and your settings (sound, text size) are stored only on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no cloud sync: if you delete the game, your progress is deleted with it.

What the game doesn't have

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase processed by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never see your payment details; the game only checks whether the purchase belongs to your account. Their own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Sharing and ratings

If you tap “Share my ending”, the game makes a picture on your device and hands it to your phone's share menu — it goes only where you choose to send it. The request to rate the game is the system's own dialog; we don't receive anything from it.

Children

The game has mature themes and is recommended for ages 16+. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: kineskop.app@gmail.com.

Коли натискаєш «Поділитися фіналом», гра створює картинку на пристрої й передає її в системне меню «Поділитися» — вона йде лише туди, куди ти сам надішлеш. Прохання оцінити гру — системне вікно; ми нічого з нього не отримуємо.

Гра має дорослі теми, рекомендовано 16+. Ми свідомо не збираємо даних ні від кого, зокрема від дітей.
