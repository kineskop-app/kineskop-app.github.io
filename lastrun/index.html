FAQ

Questions

When does it come out?

Soon — first on iPhone, then on Android. Leave your email, pick your phone, and we'll write once, when it's out there.

How much does it cost?

The prologue and chapter one are free. The rest of the story unlocks with a single purchase — no ads, no subscriptions.

Is Kamianobrid a real town?

It's a composite of Ukrainian towns. But anyone who lived through the 90s will recognise their own town in it: coupons, kiosks, the last bus at 21:40.

Is it for kids?

No. The game has violence, themes of death and alcohol. Recommended 16+.

What languages?

English and Ukrainian. The game follows your phone's language.

What data do you collect?

None. Your progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
