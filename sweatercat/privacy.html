Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sweater Cat is made by Kineskop. We don't collect any personal data that identifies you — no name, email or account.

  • Everything — your cat, timer history and settings — is stored only on your device (and in its widgets).
  • We use Google Firebase to count anonymous usage (which screens and features are used, purchases, crashes, an approximate country from the IP address, and whether the app was installed from an Apple Ads ad, via Apple's privacy-preserving AdServices) so we can improve the app. It isn't linked to you, we don't use it for advertising or tracking, and we never sell it.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no tracking.
  • App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time. The apps you choose stay on your device as private tokens — we can't see which apps they are.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Notifications are local and scheduled on your device.

If you delete the app, all its data is deleted with it.

Questions? See Support.

Вона не пов'язана з тобою, ми не використовуємо її для реклами чи стеження й ніколи не продаємо.
  • Без акаунтів, реклами й стеження.
  • Блокування застосунків працює через «Екранний час» Apple. Вибрані застосунки лишаються на пристрої як приватні токени — ми не бачимо, які це застосунки.
