Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sweater Cat is made by Kineskop. We don't collect any personal data that identifies you — no name, email or account.

  • Everything — your cat, timer history and settings — is stored only on your device (and in its widgets).
  • Focus together: when you invite a friend or join one, your cat's name and look, the room it's in and the timer (when a focus starts and how long it is) are shared through Google Firebase with that one friend, under an anonymous ID made for the app (no account). The session ends after a day, and ended sessions are deleted regularly (at least once a week). If you keep that friend on your list, your cat's name and look and whether you're focusing stay shared with them (only them) until you remove them.
  • If you write to us from Settings → "Feedback & ideas", we store the text with the app's language and version, whether you have Pro, how many sessions you've finished, your cat's name, the phone model, the iOS version, the region set on the phone and the app's anonymous ID (the same one Focus together uses, no account, so we can tell which messages came from the same phone) (Google Firebase, EU). Nothing that identifies you is attached — please don't type personal details in it. We read every message to fix and improve the app.
  • We use Google Firebase to count anonymous usage (which screens and features are used, purchases, crashes, an approximate country from the IP address, and whether the app was installed from an Apple Ads ad, via Apple's privacy-preserving AdServices) so we can improve the app. It isn't linked to you, we don't use it for advertising or tracking, and we never sell it.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no tracking.
  • App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time. The apps you choose stay on your device as private tokens — we can't see which apps they are.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Notifications are local and scheduled on your device.

If you delete the app, all its data is deleted with it.

Questions? See Support.

Якщо друг лишається у твоєму списку, ім'я й вигляд кота та чи ти зараз фокусуєшся й далі видно лише йому — доки не прибереш його зі списку.

Нічого, за чим тебе можна впізнати, не додається — будь ласка, не пиши туди особистих даних. Ми читаємо кожне повідомлення, щоб виправляти й покращувати застосунок.
  • Ми користуємося Google Firebase, щоб рахувати анонімну статистику (які екрани й функції використовують, покупки, збої, приблизна країна за IP-адресою, а також чи встановили застосунок із реклами Apple Ads — через AdServices від Apple, без стеження) і покращувати застосунок.
