Quick answers

  • Restore Pro on a new phone: Settings → Restore purchases (same Apple ID).
  • App blocking doesn't work: allow Screen Time access when asked, or turn it on again in Settings → Block apps during focus.
  • Cancel the subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sweater Cat.
  • No notifications: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Sweater Cat → Allow.
