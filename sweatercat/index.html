FAQ

Good questions

When does it come out?

Very soon on the App Store for iPhone. Leave your email and we'll write once, on launch day.

Is it really free?

Yes. The timer, the cat, the room, app blocking, gifts and widgets are free, with no ads. Pro adds extras like your own cat.

How does app blocking work?

It uses Apple's Screen Time. During focus, other apps are locked; calls always work, and you choose exceptions. We never see which apps you pick.

What if I skip a few days?

Nothing bad happens. The cat doesn't get sick or sad — it's just happy when you're back.
